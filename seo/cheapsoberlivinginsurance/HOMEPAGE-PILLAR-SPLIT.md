# Resolving the homepage / pillar collision

Two pages in the package target one query with near-identical titles and H1s
differing by a full stop. This is the copy change that separates them. Make it
**before building** — it is a five-minute edit in a draft and an expensive one
in a live index.

## Which page should move

I said in chat "fix the homepage." Reading the actual copy, **the pillar should
move instead.** The homepage is going to rank for the head term whatever we do —
it is the root of a near-exact-match domain and every internal link points at
it. Fighting that with an interior page wastes the pillar.

So: **the homepage keeps the head term. The pillar re-angles to the question its
four spokes actually answer.**

This also preserves the homepage hero copy, which is the strongest writing in
the package and should not be touched:

> A sober living residence looks like a home. It operates like a business.
> It needs insurance designed for both.

## Homepage `/` — unchanged

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| SEO title | Sober Living Home Insurance \| Property & Business Coverage |
| H1 | Insurance built for the business of sober living. |

Keep exactly as the blueprint has it.

## Pillar `/sober-living-home-insurance/` — change these three fields

| Field | Was | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| SEO title | Sober Living Home Insurance \| Specialized Business Coverage | **What Insurance a Sober Living Home Needs \| Coverage Guide** |
| Meta description | Specialized sober living home insurance for property, general liability, professional liability, business income and other operational risks. | **Which coverages a sober living home or recovery residence actually needs, why residential policies fall short, and how the pieces fit together.** |
| H1 | Insurance built for the business of sober living | **What a sober living home actually needs to be insured for** |

Add one lead paragraph under the H1, before the existing sections — it gives the
page a distinct promise and is the kind of short direct answer the build guide
asks for near the top of coverage pages:

> Most sober living homes need four things working together: property and
> business income cover for the residence, general liability for the premises,
> professional liability for the guidance and oversight the operator provides,
> and workers' compensation for house managers and staff. Which of them apply,
> and at what limits, depends on how the home is run.

The three sections already written for this page — *Home and business, covered
together*, *Designed around how your home operates*, *Avoid the residential-policy
gap* — all stay. They fit the new angle better than the old one.

## Why this works

The homepage sells the agency and converts. The pillar answers "what do I
actually need?" and routes down to the four spokes, each of which then answers
one coverage in depth. Three distinct jobs, three distinct queries, no two pages
competing.

Internal linking follows the same shape: pillar links down to all four spokes;
each spoke links back up to the pillar and across to two sibling spokes, which
is what the build guide already specifies.
