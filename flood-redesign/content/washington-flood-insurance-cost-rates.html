Washington Flood Zones and Who Needs Coverage

Washington has major flood risk from Pacific rivers (Columbia, Snake, Snoqualmie, Green) and coastal areas (Puget Sound, Washington Coast). Common zones:

  • Zone AE: High-risk riverine areas. Mandatory. Most expensive.
  • Zone A: High-risk inland. Mandatory.
  • Zone X: Low/moderate risk. Optional, but 29% of claims come from X zones.

Washington Cities and Zones

Western Washington (Wet Zone)

  • Seattle: Mix of AE (Green River), A, X
  • Tacoma: AE/A (Puyallup)
  • Olympia: AE/A (Deschutes)
  • Bellingham: AE (Nooksack)
  • Everett: X (mostly safe)

Eastern Washington & Riverside

  • Spokane: AE/A (Spokane River)
  • Walla Walla: AE (mill race)
  • Tri-Cities: A/X (Columbia)
  • Vancouver: AE (Columbia)

Western Washington Winter: Why Zone X Matters Here

Washington's winter storms and snowmelt create flash flood risk even in low-risk zones. Rivers can come out of their banks within hours. At $400-600/year for Zone X coverage, it's affordable insurance against a real seasonal risk. We've paid substantial claims on Washington Zone X properties.

FAQs

Do I need flood insurance if I'm not in a flood zone?
Not required by your lender, but recommended. 29% of claims come from moderate- and low-risk areas. Washington's terrain and winter weather make overland flooding more common than the official map shows.
