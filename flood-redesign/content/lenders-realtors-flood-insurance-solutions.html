The Problem We Solve

For Lenders

  • Compliance risk: Mandatory flood insurance in high-risk zones must be in place before loan approval. If the borrower can't get coverage or it prices them out, your deal stalls.
  • Timeline pressure: Standard flood insurance takes 30+ days (federal waiting period). You need approval before closing, which means you need to order coverage weeks in advance.
  • Rate shopping friction: Borrowers go direct to NFIP and accept the first quote without knowing private programs might be significantly cheaper — which affects their debt-to-income ratio and approval odds.
  • B-paper and refusals: Harder-to-insure properties (prior claims, non-standard construction, high-hazard areas) may not get approved by NFIP. Your deal can't close if coverage isn't available.

For Realtors

  • Last-minute gaps: Buyer's insurance agent finds out during final underwriting that flood coverage is required, wasn't addressed, and now the closing is delayed 30+ days.
  • Compliance unknowns: You don't know flood zones well enough to brief your buyers properly — leading to surprises during underwriting.
  • Pricing kills deals: Federal flood insurance quotes shock buyers into renegotiating or walking. No one budgeted an extra $800+/year.
  • It's not your job: Flood insurance is a closing necessity, not your expertise. You need a partner who handles it completely.
