## Notes on using it

**Post it now, not in October.** The whole argument is the 30-day waiting period, which only works as a
call to action while nothing is in the forecast. In November this post argues against itself.

**One GBP profile, two brands.** Per the theme constants, both sites share a single Google Business
Profile under the California Flood Insurance Services name. So this is a California-framed post on a
California-named profile — which is consistent. Don't post the statewide version there as well; it would
compete with itself on the same profile.

**Worth repeating monthly while the advisory holds.** NOAA updates ENSO on the second Thursday of each
month. If the next discussion moves the numbers materially, update the figures rather than reposting the
same ones — the specificity is what makes it credible, and stale specifics are worse than none.

**Do not post it after a local flood event.** Same text reads as opportunism in the days after damage.
Wait a week, and lead with the claims process instead.
