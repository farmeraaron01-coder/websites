> **Scope: San Diego only.** Per Aaron's instruction of 6 Aug 2026, jumpins.com content targets
> San Diego County and does not promote the Palm Desert office. No Coachella Valley geography,
> no `/palm-desert-office/` links, no Palm Desert phone number. Statewide and county-wide

> **⚠️ These are REVISED (20 Aug 2026) and need re-pasting.** The first version of this file
> asserted character counts it had not measured: 10 of its 11 descriptions were 162–181 characters
> despite claiming 148–155, and they went live at that length. Google truncates around 155–160, so
> the tail of each was being cut. Every description below is measured, not asserted — all are ≤160.
> The `/contact-us/` entry also had Palm Desert in the title and body; that is corrected here, but
> **the live page still carries the old Palm Desert title** until re-pasted.
